How the pieces were cut

The puzzle shapes are not drawn by the web page. They were made as GIS features in ArcGIS Pro, exported as GeoJSON, and lined up with the georeferenced map here.

  1. Island outlineStatistics Canada 2021 provinces, Multipart To Singlepart, island only, in NAD83(CSRS) UTM zone 21N.
  2. Storybook stylingMultiple Ring Buffer at 3, 7 and 12 km for the coastal glow; pastel fills.
  3. IconsXY Table To Point from a CSV of places, symbolized with picture markers.
  4. ExportExport Map to a 3000 × 1940 GeoTIFF with a world file.
  5. GridCreate Fishnet over the image extent: 6 columns × 5 rows.
  6. Shared edgesPolygon To Line with neighbour information, then only edges with two neighbours.
  7. Knob positionsGenerate Points Along Lines at 50%, one point per inner edge.
  8. Random ownersCalculate Field picks the left or right neighbour for each knob.
  9. KnobsPairwise Buffer of 25 km around each point.
  10. PiecesUnion with the grid, Calculate Field for piece ID, then Dissolve into 30 pieces.
  11. To the webFeatures To JSON, then projected to image pixels with the world file.
